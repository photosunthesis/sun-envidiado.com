I've been in software development for years now. I even earned a senior role recently. And to this day, I still don't quite know what the hell Agile is.

I probably should've asked at my first job. I was new then, I could've just said "wait, what do you mean by Agile?" and somebody would've explained it to me. Now I'm a senior developer, so it's way too embarrassing to ask at this point. I'm supposed to be the one who knows things.

Thank god people rarely go that deep into it in interviews. If someone asks me about a bug I fixed, yeah, I can talk about that. I can tell them what broke, what I tried first, why that didn't work. But if they asked me how we used Agile on that same project, I honestly wouldn't know what to tell them.

As a software developer, all I really know is how the work itself goes. I grab a Jira ticket. If something's broken I fix it; if it doesn't exist yet I make it. Then I test it and go, oh, okk, that part doesn't work. Back to fixing it. Eventually the thing does what the ticket says it should do and I deploy it. And in between all of that? Meetings. Lots of them, at pretty much every step.

Now if you asked me which part of my day was Agile, I think I'd say the meetings? That's a guess, by the way. Cuz even if we didn't have a meeting that day, I'd still be working on the same thing. And sometimes I wonder if it's the Jira part, but somebody could just send me a message about a bug and I'd fix it the same way. So I don't know why that would matter either.

Maybe I'm already doing it and it's just the whole thing I described. Or maybe none of that is Agile. Like, if somebody told me "yeah, that's just how you do your job, Agile is something else" I'd probably believe them. I genuinely don't know.

I mean, I'd say I'm pretty _agile_. I've gone from .NET to Laravel to Flutter for work, then picked up React and Astro for fun. Tell me we're using something else tomorrow and honestly I'd be kinda excited.

I don't think that's what they mean by it though.

So what is it, genuinely?

I know I could literally just Google it right now. I could've spent the last twenty minutes reading about it instead of writing a whole blog post about not knowing it, but then I'd actually know it, and I wouldn't have anything to write about.

Besides, I made it to senior without knowing. I think I'll be fine.
